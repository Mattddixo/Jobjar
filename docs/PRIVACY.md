# Job Jar privacy policy

_Last updated: 22 October 2026_

Job Jar is a to-do app that runs entirely on your phone. This page covers what it does with your data.

## What Job Jar stores

The jobs you create (titles, notes, time estimates, categories, priorities, repeat settings,
completion history and scheduled dates) and your settings (theme, Jar tab choices). All of it
is stored only on your device.

## What Job Jar shares

Nothing. Job Jar has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it doesn't
have permission to use the internet. No data is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## Calendar access

If you schedule a job, Job Jar asks for calendar permission and adds one event for that job to
a calendar on your phone. If that calendar is synced to an account (for example Google
Calendar), your phone's own sync sends the event there, the same as any event you create
yourself. Job Jar reads your calendars only to find one it can write to, and to check that an
event it is about to change or delete is one it created. It never reads the contents of your
other events.

## Backups and exports

- **Android backup:** if you have device backup turned on, Android may include Job Jar's data
  in your encrypted device backup, as it does for most apps.
- **Export:** Settings → Export jobs saves a file wherever you choose. What happens to that
  file after that is up to you.

## Deleting your data

Delete jobs in the app, or uninstall Job Jar to remove all of its data from your phone. Events
already added to your calendar stay there until you delete them.
