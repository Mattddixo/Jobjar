# Installing Job Jar on an iPhone

Job Jar isn't on the App Store, so it's installed from a computer with a free tool called
Sideloadly. It takes about 15 minutes the first time.

You'll need:

- An iPhone on iOS 17 or newer (Settings > General > About > iOS Version)
- A Windows PC or a Mac, and your iPhone's cable
- Your Apple ID (the one you use for the App Store)

## 1. Set up the computer (once)

**Windows:** install iTunes and iCloud from Apple's website, not the Microsoft Store versions:

- iTunes: https://www.apple.com/itunes/download/win64
- iCloud for Windows: search "iCloud for Windows download" on apple.com

Sideloadly's download page links to the right versions too. Then install Sideloadly from
https://sideloadly.io.

**Mac:** just install Sideloadly from https://sideloadly.io.

## 2. Download the app

Go to https://github.com/Mattddixo/Jobjar/releases, open the newest "Job Jar for iPhone"
release and download `JobJar.ipa`.

## 3. Install it

1. Plug the iPhone into the computer. If it asks "Trust this computer?", tap Trust.
2. Open Sideloadly. Your iPhone should show up at the top.
3. Drag `JobJar.ipa` onto the Sideloadly window.
4. Type your Apple ID email and click **Start**. Enter your Apple ID password when asked
   (it goes to Apple, to sign the app for your phone).
5. Wait for "Done".

## 4. Allow it on the iPhone (once)

1. **Settings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode**: turn it on. The phone restarts; after
   it's back, tap Turn On.
2. **Settings > General > VPN & Device Management**: tap your Apple ID under Developer App
   and tap Trust.

Job Jar is now on the home screen.

## Every 7 days

Apps installed this way with a free Apple ID stop opening after 7 days. Your jobs aren't lost.
Plug the phone in and do step 3 again with the same file. Sideloadly also has an automatic
refresh option that can do this over Wi-Fi while the computer is on.

## Updates

When there's a new version, download the new `JobJar.ipa` from the releases page and install
it the same way. Your jobs stay put.
